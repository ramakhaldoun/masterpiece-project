About Ma’aky

A place made for the long road

Cancer changes your days, your body and sometimes your sense of who you are. Ma’aky (معكِ, “with you”) is here so you don’t carry it quietly.

Be heard

Small, moderated circles and private messages with women who understand, with nothing to explain.

Move gently

Soft yoga, art and walks in Amman, shaped around how you feel that day, not how you “should”.

Never alone

Nour, your companion, listens when it’s heavy and suggests something kind based on your mood.

Your daily needs

Find, borrow or share everyday care essentials, from recovery pillows to comfort items that make each day a little easier.

Understand your journey

Trusted, easy-to-understand information about treatment, side effects and preparing for appointments.

Support near you

Helplines, clinics and support groups across Jordan, gathered in one place.

Support

With you at every stage

Diagnosis, treatment, recovery, remission, or supporting someone you love. Wherever you are on the journey, there is a community made for you here.
